# Route B: from Arthur's local trace formula

Planned: a short, self-contained guide showing how the main identity of the paper follows from Arthur's local trace formula (Publ. Math. IHÉS 73, 1991) when one test function is supported on the elliptic set, together with Arthur's results on elliptic tempered characters (Acta Math. 171, 1993). Intended both as an independent proof of the identity and as an introduction to Arthur's work through an explicit case.

Status (November 2026): a sketch is §7 of the revised paper (`../paper/`), including what survives of the geometric and spectral sides when one test function is spherical and the other elliptic, and the expected identification of I(χ,φ) with a weighted character (Conjecture 7.3). Normalizations are not yet checked.
