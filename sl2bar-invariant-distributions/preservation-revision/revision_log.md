# Draft 1 working revision audit

Baseline: original January 4, 1994 metaplectic source, not the ordinary SL2 rewrite. All six sections retained. This is a preservation-first working draft, not a completed corrected edition.

All historical formal claims have individually numbered unverified markers; original theorem counters are unchanged. New Section 7 gives independently derived elementary results, with new numbering. Literature abstracts consulted: https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.6134, https://www.numdam.org/item/AIF_2014__64_6_2379_0/, https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08859. Historical citations and pinpoint modern theorem references remain to be checked.

Corrections: distinguish valuation and absolute value; Borel rather than globally continuous cocycle; central equivariance and trace cocenter; necessary induction relations; circle distribution constant mode; direct sum versus product; quotient topology and kernel condition. Historical Section 6 product formula and Section 5 sufficiency claims remain explicitly unverified legacy targets, superseded where the new section gives corrections.

Style defaults: article, newtx, letter, one-inch margins. Legacy wording, proof sketches, citation keys, cross-references and some style violations remain for a subsequent audited pass. No claim of complete verification.
