# sl2bar-trace/revised

The 2026 revision of *A hitch-hiker's guide to the metaplectic trace formula for SL(2)*, restricted to the double cover (n = 2). It is written section by section, following the stages in [`../revision_log.md`](../revision_log.md). Authors: D. Joyner and Claude. The draft of 2026-11-11 answers the referee report in [`../referee-2026-11/`](../referee-2026-11/). A later pass the same day closed the open items of that round (the condition (N5), the citations, the stable-sum statement); see the last section of the revision log. Title: *A hitch-hiker's guide to the metaplectic trace formula for SL(2): the double cover*.

|---|---|

| `sec7-stable.tex` | Section 7 (stage 6): the action of GL(2) on the cover (Ad(h), explicit on N, A, the center, K, tori and G(ℚ)); the sign (det h, det(1+γ)) by which the centralizer of γ in GL(2) acts on the lifts of γ; stable conjugacy as the twisted action h⋆x and the function Δ_ψ that is constant on stable classes; the kernel of the Weil representation in Kubota's model and the character of r⁺ − r⁻; stable and κ-orbital integrals, with J^κ(x, f) = J^st(−x, f₋); the regular elliptic terms as S_ell(f) + S_ell(f₋); the behavior of I_A under GL(2); the unipotent coefficients a^G(S,u_ξ) = ½(c_S + Σ_κ κ_S(ξ) L^S(1,κ)); the action of GL(2,k) on the spectrum and the trace on the theta functions; the stable(r) form and what is missing for a stabilization. The statements about the weighted terms use the property (N5) of the normalizing factors; Proposition 7.8′ constructs factors with (N1)–(N5) at every finite place (from the scalar M(π^w,−λ)M(π,λ)), and by Szpruch's local coefficients they are a constant times L(2λ,χ²)/L(2λ+1,χ²), also at the dyadic and ramified places |
