\title{A hitch-hiker's guide to the metaplectic trace formula for $SL(2)$\\[2pt]
\large Sections 2--4, revised for the double cover (draft)}
\author{David Joyner and Claude}
\date{November 2026}
\maketitle

\noindent These are the revised Sections~2--4 of the 1995 notes,
restricted to $n=2$, compiled on their own. In Section~2, Lemmas~2.1--2.9
keep the numbers of the 1995 notes and Lemma~2.10 is new. Section~3 is
shorter than in 1995, because for $n=2$ the cover of a torus is abelian.
Section~4 merges the 1995 Section~4 with the file \texttt{cctrac3b.tex}
that the 1995 master inputs (it is Chapter~9.3--9.5 of the 1997 book
manuscript), and proves the coarse geometric expansion directly for this
group. The old and new numbers are matched in \texttt{revision\_log.md},
which also lists the changes.
