\title{A hitch-hiker's guide to the metaplectic trace formula for $SL(2)$\\[2pt]
\large Sections 2--6, revised for the double cover (draft)}
\author{David Joyner and Claude}
\date{November 2026}
\maketitle

\noindent These are the revised Sections~2--6 of the 1995 notes,
restricted to $n=2$, compiled on their own. In Section~2, Lemmas~2.1--2.9
keep the numbers of the 1995 notes and Lemma~2.10 is new. Section~3 is
shorter than in 1995, because for $n=2$ the cover of a torus is abelian.
Section~4 merges the 1995 Section~4 with the file \texttt{cctrac3b.tex}
that the 1995 master inputs (it is Chapter~9.3--9.5 of the 1997 book
manuscript), and proves the coarse geometric expansion directly for this
group. Section~5 adds the residual spectrum (even Weil representations)
and the ramified classes to the spectral side, normalizes the
intertwining operator, and corrects $M_B^T$. Section~6 puts the trace
formula in invariant form: for this group the invariant distributions
$I_A(\gamma,f)$ are constructed by Fourier analysis on the abelian cover
of $A$, the global normalizing factor is identified, and the ramified
term is evaluated ($M(w,0)=-1$). The old and new numbers are matched in
\texttt{revision\_log.md}, which also lists the changes.
