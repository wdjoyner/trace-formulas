# Genuine invariant distributions on metaplectic covers of SL(2)

**David Joyner**, *A hitch-hiker's guide to genuine invariant distributions on metaplectic covers of SL(2) over a p-adic field*.

**Current manuscript:** Draft 2 (November 21, 2026), a corrected and expanded synthesis of the January 4, 1994 manuscript and the SL(2) material relevant to the author's 2001 *Journal of Fourier Analysis and Applications* article. The current paper excludes the GL(r) theory.

## Review status

This is an **expository working draft**, not an independently refereed article. The topological Paley--Wiener step is explicitly conditional, and the manuscript records unresolved normalization and endpoint-intertwining assertions. The published 2001 article is cited, not reproduced.

AI assistance by ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic) is identified in the first sentence of the abstract and explained in the Acknowledgments.

Following the `trace-formulas` convention, file names do not carry draft numbers; Git history tracks successive drafts.
